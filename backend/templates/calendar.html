Sowing Calendar — Maharashtra

Month-wise guide for optimal sowing, planting, and harvesting of major crops in Maharashtra.

Kharif Season (June - November): Main sowing starts after pre-monsoon showers. Ensure soil has adequate moisture before sowing.
Cotton (BT कापूस)
Sowing TimeJune 1st - July 15
HarvestingOctober - February
VarietyBG-II, NHH-44
DistrictsVidarbha, Marathwada
Apply Urea at 30 DAS for early boll formation.
Soybean (सोयाबीन)
Sowing TimeJune 15 - July 15
HarvestingOctober - November
VarietyJS-335, MAUS-71
DistrictsAll Maharashtra
Seed treatment with Rhizobium culture improves yield by 15%.
Rice (भात)
NurseryJune 1 - June 20
TransplantingJuly 1 - August 15
VarietyIndrayani, PKV Sona
DistrictsKonkan, Nashik
Maintain 5cm water level during tillering stage.
Tur / Pigeon Pea (तूर)
Sowing TimeJune 15 - July 15
HarvestingJanuary - February
VarietyICPL-87, BDN-2
DistrictsLatur, Solapur
Use weed control at 20-25 DAS for maximum grain yield.
Rabi Season (October - March): Sown after monsoon. Primarily depends on residual soil moisture or irrigation.
Wheat (गहू)
Sowing TimeNov 1 - Dec 15
HarvestingMarch - April
VarietyNW-1014, K-307
Irrigation4-5 irrigations needed
First irrigation (Crown Root Initiation) is most critical at 21 DAS.
Onion (कांदा)
NurserySept 15 - Oct 15
TransplantingNov - Dec
HarvestingFebruary - March
VarietyN-2-4-1, Bhima Raj
Stop irrigation 15 days before harvest to improve storage life.
Gram / Chickpea (हरभरा)
Sowing TimeOct 15 - Nov 15
HarvestingFebruary - March
VarietyVishal, PKV-2
DistrictMarathwada, Vidarbha
Single irrigation at pre-flowering is highly beneficial.
Summer Season (February - May): Irrigation-dependent crops. Requires careful water management.
Groundnut (भुईमूग)
Sowing TimeJan 15 - Feb 28
HarvestingMay - June
VarietyJL-24, Phule Pragati
Gypsum application at 200 kg/acre helps pod filling.
Sunflower (सूर्यफूल)
Sowing TimeJan 15 - Mar 1
HarvestingMay
VarietyMSFH-17, CO-1
Hand pollination during morning hours increases seed set.
